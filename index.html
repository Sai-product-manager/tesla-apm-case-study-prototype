Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Tesla Energy & Fleet Sync (TEFS)

Prepared for Tesla Associate Product Manager (APM) Hiring Committee • Vehicle Software & Energy Ecosystem

1 Problem Statement (First Principles)

Tesla owners rely on static scheduled departure times. When meetings shift or plans delay, vehicles still execute high-wattage cabin heating/cooling for an empty car—wasting 1.5–3.2 kWh per incident and creating synchronized morning grid spikes across millions of Superchargers and residential Powerwalls.

2 Product Vision & Strategic Fit

Transform the Tesla App from a reactive remote control into a proactive energy orchestrator. By pairing zero-knowledge on-device calendar telemetry with Tesla Energy Virtual Power Plant (VPP) grid signals, TEFS maximizes range while reducing peak utility strain.

Phase 1 • Weeks 1–3

Internal Employee Dogfooding

Deploy via early-access firmware to Fremont, Austin, and Palo Alto employee fleets to tune commute vs. all-day calendar event filtering.

Phase 2 • Weeks 4–6

Solar + Powerwall Beta Cohort

Roll out to 50,000 multi-product homes to validate excess solar thermal pre-conditioning before grid TOU peak windows.

Phase 3 • Week 8+

Global OTA Software Release

Full fleet OTA update with Bluetooth RSSI primary-driver phone key prioritization and 1-tap snooze controls.

Privacy & Multi-Driver Architecture

Zero-Knowledge Calendar Parsing: Meeting titles, attendees, and locations never leave the user's phone. Local Edge ML computes only an anonymized timestamp (departure_target_utc) and binds it to the primary driver's phone key via Bluetooth RSSI proximity.
